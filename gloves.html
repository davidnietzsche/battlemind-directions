20 · Red Gloves — the fight experience

On load

The three headline lines punch in one after the other, each landing with a small impact ring and a 60 ms screen nudge. She fades up last and settles into her guard. The button appears with the third line.

Mouse

Two parallax depths: the headline moves a little, she moves more and leans toward the cursor like a boxer tracking an opponent. The cursor is a crosshair, an invitation.

Click / tap

Anywhere on the hero throws a jab: a glove flies from her guard to the click point, a red shockwave ripples out across the page (WebGL), the headline wobbles, and the glove snaps back. Visitors play with it; nobody has to explain the brand.

Scroll

The hero pins for one extra screen. The headline lines slide apart, she steps to the edge, and three bell ticks reveal the three service rounds in the dark section below, each sliding in with its own impact ring.

WebGL layer

A single fullscreen fragment shader draws the shockwaves: concentric rings with a refractive displacement of the white background, in brand red, fading in 900 ms. It costs almost nothing and runs on any phone. The gloves, type and photo stay DOM.

Fallbacks

  • No WebGL: the ring is a CSS border circle that scales and fades, same timing.
  • Reduced motion: no pin, no shake, the rings appear without animation.
  • Phones: tap = jab, she moves to the right edge, headline above her.
