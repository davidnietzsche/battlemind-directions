32 · Gachapon — zoom out, then dispense

On load

You open on the pile: the inside of the dome fills the whole screen, capsules, toy characters and red marketing objects jammed together, with the headline on top. Nothing else is visible yet.

Scroll 1 · zoom out

The first scroll pulls the camera back. The pile shrinks into the glass dome, the whole red machine appears on the right, and the headline moves to the left column with the intro copy and the button.

Scroll 2 · dispense

Every further scroll turns the machine: it shakes, a capsule drops out of the chute, bounces, splits open, and a service pops out with its name. Four capsules for four services. Each one then slides into a tray along the bottom, so by the end of the hero the service menu is sitting there in capsules.

Mouse

The pile and the machine follow the cursor slightly, which keeps the opening frame alive before anyone scrolls.

Headline

Two options are wired in, switch with the pill at the bottom right: “Human first. Results always.” and “Win the right battles. Scale results.”

Build

One pinned section, one scrubbed timeline (GSAP). The machine and the pile are two images; capsules are CSS, the services are the same red toy icons used elsewhere on the site. No WebGL, so it runs on phones; on phones the hero shows the machine and the four capsules without the pin.
