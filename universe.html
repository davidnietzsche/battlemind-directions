22 + 21 · the universe, on red

On load

The page is already red. Eighteen pieces pop out of the centre one after another and settle into a wide, airy orbit: white objects, black toy characters. The two headline lines rise out of their slots, the white button lands last.

Mouse

The whole ring tilts and shifts with the cursor, and the headline drifts a little less, so the orbit reads as a real 3D object. Pieces near the cursor get nudged aside and float back when it leaves.

Scroll

The hero is pinned for one extra screen. Scrolling spins the ring faster and sends it outward past the edges, so you fly through the universe into the white section below, where the four services land as cards with their red toy icons, and a character peeks in from the right.

Headline

Two options are wired in, switch with the pill at the bottom right: Sue's new line “Human first. Results always.” and the current site line “Win the right battles. Scale results.” Any line fits the same choreography.

Build

No WebGL: the orbit is 18 transparent images positioned by one small script, so it runs at 60 fps on phones and the text stays selectable and indexable. The pieces are the same toy set that reappears through the site (cards, 404, loaders).

Fallbacks

  • Reduced motion: the ring is drawn once, still; cards simply appear.
  • Phones: smaller ring, same orbit, no pin.
